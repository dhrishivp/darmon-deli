Our story

Cosy, chic and a little bit Gallic

Darmon opened its doors in March 2020, days before the first lockdown, and has since become one of Amersham's favourite places to eat. Behind the red shopfront you'll find deep navy banquettes, botanical wallpaper and lamplit tables, with Eldad and the team looking after you.

Lunch brings French toasties, burgers on our own bread and bistro mains. In the evening the board takes over, with a meat, fish and vegetarian dish of the day. The pâtisseries are all made in our own kitchen with pure butter.

  • Bistro classics plus daily du jour specials
  • Homemade bread, desserts & pâtisserie
  • Vegetarian and gluten-free options
  • Takeaway available, just call ahead
